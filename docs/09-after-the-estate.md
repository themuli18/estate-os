# 9. After the estate: the survivor's administration

Once the inheritance is settled, the repository doesn't get archived. It becomes the surviving partner's household
administration. The scanning, mail monitoring, index and deadline discipline all carry on. What changes is the
emphasis.

## What to change in the repository

1. **`CLAUDE.md` header and §0.** The purpose becomes "administratie van [PARTNER]". The estate facts stay as
   history in a section "Nalatenschap (afgerond)".
2. **Close the estate workstreams.** Move §5 A–D and G into the archive section. Keep E (protection) and F (taxes),
   and add §5 H "Jaarkalender".
3. **Contract register.** Promote the bank workbook's "recurring" tab into
   `Documenten/00_Contractenregister.md`: per contract the provider, number, holder, amount and frequency, end date or
   notice period, where the policy sheet is, and the next decision date.
4. **TODO sections** become: Deze maand · Jaarkalender · Contracten · Gezondheid en zorg · Open vragen.
5. **Plain-language layer.** Add a folder `Voor [PARTNER]/` with one-page summaries: "Wie bel ik als …", "Wat
   betaal ik elke maand", "Waar ligt …". Print them and keep them in a physical folder too.

## Yearly calendar (Netherlands: verify dates each year)

| Month | What |
|---|---|
| January | new insurance premiums and policy sheets arrive (file them); annual statements from pension funds and SVB; WOZ assessment and municipal taxes |
| Feb–Apr | annual statements from banks; **income tax return** (usually due 1 May unless an extension was granted); check provisional allowance amounts (toeslagen) |
| Spring | gemeentelijke belastingen / waterschapsbelasting payment arrangement; insurance review |
| Summer | holiday allowance arrives with the pension; check that the travel insurance matches how the survivor actually travels |
| Autumn | **health insurance**: decide before year-end whether to switch for 1 January; energy contract renewal date (from the register) |
| December | check direct debits against the contract register; plan the next year's TODO |
| Every year | re-read the levenstestament and will; are the authorised people still right? |

Every recurring item goes into the survivor's calendar with an instruction in the description. The mail sweep also
picks up the reminders that providers send.

## Protecting the survivor

- **Levenstestament** (notarial) for financial and medical decisions, and a written medical directive. This is the
  single most important document if the survivor's health declines.
- **Authorisations instead of shared passwords:** DigiD Machtigen for government services, bank authorisation
  forms for a trusted person.
- **A "noodmap"** (emergency folder): one page with GP, medication, insurer, the bank's fraud line, the authorised
  persons, and where the original documents are.
- **Fraud watch.** Recently bereaved people are targeted. Train the triage rule that any unexpected request for
  payment or codes is *urgent: verify by phone using a number from our own §8, not from the message*.

## Handover

If the coordinator role passes to someone else, the new person clones the repo, reads `CLAUDE.md` §2a and the last
month of `LOG.md`, and is up to date. This is the main payoff of journaling everything.
